How to read this

  • Player, goalkeeper, referee and ball detection uses football-trained YOLOv8 models; the pitch keypoint model maps the image onto a 120 × 70 m pitch. Distances, speeds, heatmaps, shots and goals use only calibrated frames.
  • Only people and balls inside the touch and goal lines count. One referee and one goalkeeper per end are kept per frame.
  • Possession goes to the player within 1.5 m of the ball. A pass is the ball moving between two players of a team in one camera shot.
  • A shot is the ball leaving a player at 43 km/h or more towards a goal from within 30 m. Shot speed is measured on the ground plane, so a lofted shot reads faster than it was. A goal is the ball seen inside the goal mouth; shots shown in close-up or from behind the goal are missed, and a high shot over the bar can be miscounted.
  • The same goal shown again within 60 s (replays) counts once, but replayed shots still count again.
